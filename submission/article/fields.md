AETHER: AI Recruitment Decision Support with Strands Decider 2B

Description (max 512 characters):
AETHER evaluates candidate resumes against job requirements using Strands Decider 2B for every typed compatibility decision. One forward pass returns advance, review, or decline with a calibrated confidence score. Amazon Bedrock handles conversation and explanation. Missing information is always treated as unknown, never as proof of absence. The final hiring call always stays with the recruiter.
